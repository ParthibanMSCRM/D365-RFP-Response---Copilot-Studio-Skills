Executive Summary

Example Customer Group requires a modern customer engagement platform to improve sales productivity, customer service quality, pipeline visibility and management reporting. The proposed solution uses Dynamics 365 Sales, Dynamics 365 Customer Service, Dataverse, Power Platform and Microsoft Entra ID to provide a secure, scalable and governed business application platform.

Proposed Solution

We propose a Dynamics 365 Customer Engagement solution that gives sales, service and leadership teams a single governed platform for customer records, activities, pipeline, cases, service interactions and reporting.

Capability Proposed approach
Sales management Use Dynamics 365 Sales for accounts, contacts, leads, opportunities, activities, pipeline tracking and forecasting.
Sales productivity Evaluate Sales accelerator, sequences and guided selling features where licensing and business process fit are confirmed.
Customer service Use Dynamics 365 Customer Service for case management, queues, service routing, knowledge articles and SLA tracking.
Data platform Use Microsoft Dataverse as the core business data platform with role-based access and auditable data changes.
Automation Use Power Automate for approvals, notifications, task automation and integration triggers.
Reporting Use Dynamics 365 dashboards and Power BI for operational reporting, executive dashboards and trend analysis.
Identity and access Use Microsoft Entra ID for identity, single sign-on and conditional access subject to tenant policy.

Implementation Approach

Phase Indicative duration Key outcomes

Configuration and build 4 weeks Configure Dynamics 365 apps, Dataverse tables, forms, views, workflows, dashboards and security roles.

Risks and Assumptions

Area Risk or assumption Mitigation
Licensing Required Dynamics 365 and Power Platform licenses must be confirmed. Validate licensing during discovery and confirm any premium feature dependencies.
Sales accelerator Capability availability may depend on product licensing and tenant configuration. Validate against Microsoft Learn and target tenant configuration before final commitment.
Integration External system APIs and data ownership are not yet confirmed. Complete integration discovery and confirm API access, security and environments.
Data migration Historical data quality may affect migration effort. Run sample profiling, cleansing and test migration before final migration estimate.
Change management User adoption may be impacted if business process changes are not managed. Use role-based training, champion users, communication planning and adoption reporting.
